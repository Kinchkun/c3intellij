Reports declarations that are never used: structs, unions, interfaces, enums, typedefs, aliases, faults, functions, methods, global variables, constants, function parameters and local variables. Unused declarations are greyed out and can be removed with the Delete unused declaration quick fix.

Entry points and externally-visible declarations are not reported: the main function, declarations carrying attributes such as @export, @extern, @cname, @init or @test, and methods (with their parameters) that satisfy an interface implemented by their receiver type.
